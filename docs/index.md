## Welcome to Kyoushitsu B!

Kyoushitsu B is a Wiki Database for Mobile Rhythm Game Guides!

The purpose of this database is to provide you with the best information to get better at your favorite rhythm game, along with resources to make your rhythm game experience the best it can possibly be! You will find information about specific pattern names, strategies to improve on these specific patterns, among other rhythm game related ideas. There will also be guides to optimize your device to make sure it is running at its best for rhythm games!
